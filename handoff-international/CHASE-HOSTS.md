# Chasing the 22 clubs with no day or time

> **Sequencing note (2026-11-19).** Do not send any of this yet. The invite
> system on the new site already does this job better — a host logs in and
> fixes their own club, with no transcribing by hand. See
> `HOST-CONFIRMATION.md`. Chase hosts *after* that is live and there is a
> clear way for them to plug themselves in.
>
> What survives regardless is §4: the clubs with no host on record. No login
> helps a club nobody runs.

**Generated 2026-11-19.** Source list: `ROSTER-GAPS.md` §C.

These clubs are on the site with a blank schedule. Someone can find their city
and still not know when to turn up, which is the one thing the site exists to
tell them.

**The approach that works:** show each host what we already have and ask them to
correct it. Not "please send us your details" — an open question is work, and
work gets postponed. A wrong line in front of someone gets fixed in ten seconds.

Send the group message first. It will clear a chunk of these on its own, and
then the DMs are a much shorter list.

---

## 1. The group message — hosts WhatsApp

> Morning all — quick favour, and a visible one.
>
> We're moving Breakfast Club onto the new site, and in doing the tidy-up it
> turns out **22 clubs have no day or time on record**. Not lost in the
> move — they were never filled in. So right now someone can find your city and
> still not know when to show up.
>
> I'll DM the ones affected with what we've got. If you get one, just reply with
> the corrected line — takes a few seconds:
>
> `Venue — how often — time`
> e.g. `Caravelle, Carrer del Pintor Fortuny 31 — first Wednesday — 8:30am`
>
> If your club is listed right already, nothing to do. And if you've stopped
> running yours, just say so — no awkwardness, I'd rather the map was honest.
>
> Thanks 🙏
